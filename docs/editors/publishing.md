# Drafts and publishing

## Drafts

Saving an entry that is live saves a **draft** — the published page does not change until you publish. A draft
can be previewed as the site will render it, with a bar across the top saying what you are looking at.

## Revisions

Every publish keeps a revision. The revision list shows who changed what and when, and any revision can be
restored, which creates a new draft rather than overwriting what is live.

## Scheduling

An entry can be published at a future time. A job runs every minute and publishes whatever is due, so a scheduled
entry needs nothing from you at the time.

## Review

Where a site turns it on, an entry moves through **review** before it can be published: someone with the right
role approves it, and the approval is recorded with the entry's history.

## Unpublishing

Unpublishing takes an entry off the site and keeps it. As with a slug change, the URL is left pointing somewhere
sensible rather than breaking.
