# Forms

A form's fields and validation are part of the site's schema; what arrives is yours to read.

## Submissions

Each form has a list of submissions, with search and filters, and can be exported. Uploaded files arrive as
assets attached to the submission.

## Email

A form can email someone when it is submitted. Who it goes to is part of the form's definition; the address it is
sent *from* is a site setting, under Integrations.

## Spam

Forms can require a CAPTCHA, configured under Integrations, and Nibble rate-limits submissions regardless.

## Keeping them

Submissions are purged on a schedule the site sets, so a form does not accumulate personal data forever.
