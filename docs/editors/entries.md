# Entries

An entry is one piece of content — a page, a guide, a post. Entries live in **collections**, and each collection
decides what its entries are made of.

The fields on the form are not fixed by Nibble: they come from a **blueprint**, which the site's developer
defines. A collection may have more than one, so "Guide" and "Gallery" can be different shapes in the same place.

## The listing

Each collection has a listing with search, filters and columns you can choose. What you see is what you may act
on: a role that cannot publish sees no publish button.

## Writing

The form is grouped into tabs and sections. Rich text has the usual formatting, links, images and tables, and
**find and replace**. Some fields relate to other content — an author, a region, a set of related guides — and
open a picker rather than a text box.

Changes are yours until you publish them. See [Drafts and publishing](publishing.md).

## URLs

An entry's URL comes from its collection's route and its slug. **Change a slug on a published entry and Nibble
records a redirect from the old URL automatically**, so nothing you have already shared breaks.

## Deleting

Deleting moves an entry to the **trash**, where it can be restored. Trash is emptied on a schedule set by the
site, 30 days by default.
